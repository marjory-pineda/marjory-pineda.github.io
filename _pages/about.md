## About Me

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in the Human-Centered Computing (HCC) Program at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC). My advisor is <a href="https://ykotturi.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Yasmine Kotturi</a>. I received my M.S. in Human-Centered Computing in 2024 and my B.A. in Business Technology Administration in 2022, both from UMBC. My research bridges community-engaged practices, human-centered AI, and accessibility. I'm particularly interested in investigating how emerging technologies, such as generative AI, both empower and fail marginalized communities.
